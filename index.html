🤔 What is gem-x.cpp?

gem-x.cpp is a powerful yet easy-to-use application that transforms how you see movement. Simply point your camera at yourself or load a video, and watch as gem-x.cpp instantly creates a detailed 3D skeleton that tracks every move you make. Whether you're analyzing your golf swing, studying dance moves, or just having fun with animation, gem-x.cpp brings your movements into the digital world with stunning accuracy.

This software is built on NVIDIA's cutting-edge GEM-X technology, converted to run smoothly on Windows systems. It's perfect for fitness enthusiasts, animators, researchers, and anyone curious about how technology can understand human motion.
